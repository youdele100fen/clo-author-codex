Agents and Roles

The Worker, Critic, Referee, and Infrastructure Roles

How Roles Work in Codex

The Codex version of the Clo-Author keeps the same role design as the Claude version, but the runtime interpretation is different.

Instead of depending on a Claude-only agent registry, Codex uses:

  • skill-level workflow entry points
  • prompt roles for workers, critics, and referees
  • hooks and scripts for automatic nudges
  • saved reports and logs as the durable audit trail

The important thing that stayed the same is the separation of powers: creators create, critics review, referees pressure-test, and infrastructure coordinates.

Discovery Roles

Librarian

Collects and synthesizes literature, maps the frontier, and positions the project in the conversation.

librarian-critic

Checks for missing literatures, recency gaps, and skewed coverage.

Explorer

Searches for datasets, evaluates feasibility, and links data options to the research design.

explorer-critic

Checks measurement validity, sample fit, and identification compatibility.

Strategy Roles

Strategist

Designs the empirical strategy, assumptions, falsification tests, and robustness plan.

strategist-critic

Stress-tests the identification logic, inference, and credibility of the design.

Execution Roles

Data-engineer

Handles data cleaning, variable construction, merges, and output-ready intermediate assets.

Coder

Implements the main analysis, robustness checks, and result-producing scripts.

coder-critic

Reviews reproducibility, strategy alignment, and code quality.

Writer

Drafts or revises manuscript sections, preserving notation, evidence alignment, and empirical-paper structure.

writer-critic

Checks claims, notation, LaTeX quality, and whether the prose outruns the evidence.

Peer Review Roles

Editor

Screens the paper, frames the review route, and synthesizes final editorial-style decisions.

domain-referee

Reviews contribution, literature positioning, and substantive relevance.

methods-referee

Reviews identification, estimation, inference, and robustness.

These roles remain especially useful near submission or major revision milestones.

Presentation and Infrastructure Roles

Storyteller

Builds talk structures and presentation artifacts from the paper.

storyteller-critic

Checks slide narrative, readability, and fidelity to the paper.

Verifier

Confirms whether compilation, packaging, and artifact-level checks actually pass.

Orchestrator

Coordinates the broader workflow: planning, routing, review pressure, and quality-gate logic.

Why the Role Design Matters

The role system exists to prevent self-approval.

Worker creates
-> Critic reviews
-> Worker fixes
-> Critic re-checks

That loop makes it harder for the system to say “looks good” to its own unchecked output.
